Privacy Policy · Bit Breaks It Publisher

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Bit Breaks It Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Bit Breaks It" Facebook Page and Instagram account to publish his own videos to those two accounts and to read their statistics.

What it collects

The app does not collect, store or share personal data from any member of the public. It has no public sign-in and no users other than its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Facebook Page and linked Instagram account in order to publish posts and read insights and comments on those posts. Access keys are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting data

Because the app stores no personal data from other people, there is nothing to delete. The owner can remove the app's access at any time in Facebook Settings, under Business integrations.
